A few useful details

Before you begin.

Does it work offline?

After online activation and downloading Apple’s language packs, capture, annotation, and Apple translation can work offline. A cached license remains usable during temporary network failures; the app periodically revalidates it. External models need their own connection.

Where do my screenshots go?

Screenshots and OCR stay on your Mac. If you choose a model provider, recognized text goes to that configured endpoint. Licensing sends a random installation ID and receipt; it does not read hardware serial numbers.

Is the source open?

Yes. The code is available under the MIT license, including permission for commercial use. The official binary and hosted activation service use activation codes; the MIT license does not grant access to that service.

What should I expect from translation?

Dense layouts, formulas, and specialist terminology may need manual checking. Compare with the original or open the text view for the complete result. macOS 26 or later is required.
